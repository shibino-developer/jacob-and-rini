Our Story

How We Met

Our story began in a rather unexpected way — with a little help from a matrimony website. Perhaps even the most beautiful beginnings need a little introduction! 😊 We came across each other's profiles, found something that caught our interest, and decided to meet. What began as a simple meeting soon became a meaningful conversation — sharing our thoughts, dreams, values, and hopes for the future. As we spoke and got to know one another, we found ourselves drawn closer by the things we shared and the differences we came to appreciate. Looking back, we believe that our meeting was not simply a matter of chance, but a quiet answer to prayers we had each carried in our hearts.

Our Journey

As our conversations continued, something beautiful began to grow between us. We shared our hopes and dreams, our prayers and expectations, and slowly began to see a future we could walk towards together. With every step, we felt God's gentle presence guiding our journey. What began with two profiles and a first meeting gradually became a relationship built on faith, understanding, and the hope of a life together.

The Engagement

We then brought our thoughts to our families, seeking their love, prayers, and blessings. By God's grace, their hearts were also drawn towards this union, and both families came together in February 2026 to take the journey forward. With faith in God's providence and the blessings of our families, 9 November 2026 was chosen as the day we would begin our life together.

The Wedding Day

What began with a simple introduction became something far more beautiful — a journey we believe God had already written into His plan for us. And as we step into this new chapter, we do so with grateful hearts, trusting the One who brought us together and praying that He will guide every step of the journey ahead. 🤍
