Our Story

How We Met

It all began with a little help from a matrimony website! 😊 Two profiles crossed paths, curiosity led to a meeting, and one conversation led to another. As we shared our dreams, values, and hopes, we discovered something truly special. What seemed like a simple coincidence slowly felt like God’s beautiful way of bringing two hearts together.

Our Journey

As our conversations grew, so did something beautiful between us. We shared our dreams, prayers, and hopes, slowly discovering a future we could build together. With every step, we felt God gently guiding our journey—from two profiles and a first meeting to a relationship rooted in faith, understanding, and love.

The Engagement

With our hearts full of hope, we shared our journey with our families, seeking their love, prayers, and blessings. By God’s grace, their hearts too embraced our union, and both families came together in February 2026 to take this beautiful journey forward. With God’s providence and our families’ blessings, 9 November 2026 was chosen as the day our forever begins.

The Wedding Day

What began as a simple introduction became a beautiful journey we believe God had lovingly written for us. With grateful hearts, we step into this new chapter, trusting the One who brought us together and praying for His guidance in every step of the journey ahead. 🤍
